### Optimizing IT Support Operations Through Data-Driven Decision Making Using Microsoft Excel

> An end-to-end Business Intelligence case study analyzing nearly **100,000 IT support tickets** to identify operational bottlenecks, evaluate agent performance, and recommend strategic investments that improve service efficiency and customer satisfaction.

---

# Executive Summary

Modern organizations rely heavily on efficient IT support operations to maintain productivity and employee satisfaction. As ticket volumes continue to grow, IT departments must determine whether improvements should come from hiring additional staff, enhancing employee training, or investing in better technology.

In this project, I analyzed **97,498 IT support tickets collected over five years (2016–2020)** using Microsoft Excel to evaluate operational performance, identify inefficiencies, and provide data-driven recommendations for improving IT service delivery. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Rather than focusing solely on dashboard creation, this project emphasizes **business decision-making** by transforming raw operational data into actionable insights.

---

# Business Problem

An organization's IT department faces increasing ticket volumes while striving to maintain fast response times and high customer satisfaction.

Management wants to answer several strategic questions:

- Should future investments prioritize hiring additional IT agents?
- Would employee training provide greater operational improvements?
- Is upgrading the ticket management software the highest ROI investment?
- Which request categories create the greatest operational bottlenecks?
- Which employees require additional coaching?
- How has support performance evolved over time?

# Key Business Insights

## 1. Ticket demand is increasing rapidly

Ticket volume more than doubled between 2016 and 2020, demonstrating sustained growth in support demand while the organization continued operating with a constant workforce. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1} :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

## 2. Customer satisfaction remained consistently high

Despite increasing workloads, customer satisfaction remained above 4/5 throughout the analysis period, suggesting the support team successfully maintained service quality while handling growing demand. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

## 3. Hardware requests are the largest operational bottleneck

Although Hardware accounts for the smallest share of requests, it requires the longest average resolution time (7.63 days), indicating a need for improved diagnostics, inventory availability, and workflow optimization. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

---

## 4. Login Access requests demonstrate the value of automation

Login Access issues have an average resolution time of only 0.31 days, highlighting how standardized and automatable processes can dramatically improve operational efficiency. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

---

## 5. Performance differences are driven more by capability than workload

Agent workloads are relatively balanced, yet there is a substantial gap between the fastest and slowest average resolution times. This suggests targeted coaching and knowledge sharing could yield greater benefits than simply increasing headcount. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

# Strategic Recommendations

### Priority 1

Upgrade the ticket management platform by introducing:

- Intelligent ticket routing
- Automated categorization
- Knowledge base integration
- Self-service capabilities
- AI-powered chatbot assistance

---

### Priority 2

Invest in structured agent development through:

- Targeted coaching
- Performance mentoring
- Knowledge-sharing sessions
- Standardized troubleshooting procedures

---

### Priority 3

Monitor operational KPIs continuously:

- Tickets per Agent
- Resolution Time
- SLA Compliance
- Satisfaction Score
- First Contact Resolution

---

### Priority 4

Expand the workforce only after workflow optimization has been implemented and monitored.

- Maintain high customer satisfaction
- Lower support costs
